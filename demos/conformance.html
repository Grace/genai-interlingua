The normalizer is genai-interlingua — an OpenTelemetry Collector processor and CLI, Apache-2.0. Every span it emits carries interlingua.lossy: the keys that span is not a faithful carrier of, on the span itself, so the loss above is queryable in production rather than only on this page.

There is already a first-party component for this. processor/genainormalizer has been in opentelemetry-collector-contrib since February 2026 and ships in the otelcol-contrib binary you are probably already running. If OpenInference and OpenLLMetry are the libraries you have, reach for it first — it needs no custom build and this does. What is measured here and not there is the cost: that component does not record what a mapping could not carry, and these 105 attributes are what that silence is hiding.
